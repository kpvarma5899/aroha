# Legacy spatial data

Do not load old GHMC 150-ward / 6-zone GeoJSON into the resolver.

Those layers (DataMeet, OpenCity 2022) predate:

- December 2025 delimitation (150 → 300 wards)
- 11 February 2026 trifurcation into GHMC, Cyberabad Municipal Corporation, and Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation
- End of the GHMC council term (no sitting corporators)

Showing them as current ward identity would be inaccurate.
